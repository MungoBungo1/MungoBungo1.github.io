Blood And Powder

Introduction:

Blood and Powder is my attempt at creating a grand strategy video game. The main idea is to have the player act as a faction in the campaign map and develop their territory from here and if a battle occurs a total war like battle begins. I want the game to be played in real time with pause and not turn based.


Current Development:

Soldiers in a company form up into a formation (default being a line formation). Formations are created by a parametric formula, that generates slots (postion) for each soldier. Next each soldier uses the Hungarian Algorithm to travel to their respective slot in the formation.
